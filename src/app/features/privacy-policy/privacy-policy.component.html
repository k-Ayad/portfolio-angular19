1. Introduction

The {{ appName }} application (the "Application", "we", "us" or "our") is a software tool that helps Shopify store owners confirm customer orders over WhatsApp. When a customer places an order in a connected Shopify store, the Application can send the customer a WhatsApp message asking them to confirm or reject the order, record their response, and show the result to the store owner in a dashboard.

The Application is developed and operated by {{ ownerName }}, an independent developer, and is published through k-ayad.com. It is currently in a testing / early-access (MVP) stage and is not yet a generally available commercial service.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Application may process, why it is processed, who it may be shared with, and the choices available to you. It applies to store owners who use the Application and to the customers of those stores whose order information is processed through it.

The Application is an independent product. It is not owned, operated, sponsored or endorsed by Meta Platforms, Inc. (including WhatsApp) or by Shopify Inc.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on how the Application is used, we may process the following information:

Store owner and store information

  • Store owner / account information, such as name and email address.
  • Shopify store information, such as the store name, store domain and the access credentials issued by Shopify when the store is connected.

Order and customer information

  • Shopify order information received from the connected store.
  • Customer name.
  • Customer phone number.
  • Order ID and the order details required to confirm the order (for example items, quantities, total and shipping details).
  • Order status and confirmation status.

WhatsApp messaging information

  • Information about WhatsApp messages sent and received through the Application, such as message identifiers, message content and timestamps.
  • Message delivery, read and failure status, where this is provided by Meta.
  • Confirmation or rejection actions taken by customers in response to an order-confirmation message.

Technical information

  • Technical information such as IP addresses, server and application logs, timestamps and security-related information, where applicable.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information described above only to provide and operate the Application, including to:

  • Connect and operate Shopify stores within the Application.
  • Receive, process and track orders.
  • Send WhatsApp order-confirmation messages to customers.
  • Process customers' confirmation and rejection responses.
  • Track the delivery status of WhatsApp messages.
  • Provide dashboard functionality and analytics to store owners, such as viewing orders, confirmation status and message status.
  • Maintain the security of the Application and prevent misuse.
  • Diagnose and fix technical problems.
  • Maintain and improve the Application.

4. WhatsApp Business Platform (Meta)

The Application uses Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform (WhatsApp Cloud API) to send WhatsApp messages, receive customer replies, and receive message status updates (such as sent, delivered, read or failed).

To do this, the customer's phone number and the content of the order-confirmation message are transmitted to Meta, and Meta returns message and status information to the Application. Information handled by WhatsApp and Meta is also subject to their own terms and privacy policies, including the WhatsApp Privacy Policy and the WhatsApp Business Policy.

The Application is a separate product that integrates with the WhatsApp Business Platform. It is not owned, operated or controlled by Meta.

5. Shopify

The Application integrates with Shopify. When a store owner connects their Shopify store, the Application may receive store and order information from Shopify, including through Shopify webhooks when new orders are created or updated. This information is processed only for the purpose of providing the Application's order-confirmation service to that store.

Shopify is an independent platform, and information held by Shopify is subject to Shopify's Privacy Policy. Store owners remain responsible for their relationship with their own customers, including informing them how their information is used.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share information only with the service providers and integrations needed to operate the Application:

  • Shopify — to connect stores and receive order information.
  • Meta / WhatsApp Business Platform — to send WhatsApp messages and receive replies and delivery status.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers — where applicable, to host and run the Application and store its data.

We may also disclose information where required by law, or where reasonably necessary to protect the security or integrity of the Application or the rights of its users.

7. Data Security

8. Data Retention

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Application, maintain records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and maintain security. When information is no longer needed for these purposes, it is deleted or anonymized.

9. Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your information, where applicable, by emailing {{ contactEmail }} with the subject "Data Deletion Request".

To help us locate your information, please include:

  • Store owners: the email address of your account and your Shopify store domain.
  • Customers: the phone number used for the order and, if available, the store name and order number.

We will confirm receipt of your request and delete the relevant information unless we need to keep it for a legitimate reason, such as a legal obligation, resolving a dispute or maintaining security. If you are a customer of a store, you may also contact that store directly.
